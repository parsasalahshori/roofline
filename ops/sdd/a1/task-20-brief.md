### Task 20 (track lead): rerun keeps switched-off steps (sentinel pass 9, item 3)

**Base:** `a1-agents` after Task 19 (both edit `apps/api/src/tasks/service.ts`).

**Problem:** `rerunTask` copies only `{ type, payload }` for each step. Since S1, a draft can hold steps saved switched off (`enabled = false`, `errorPlain` = the plain reason, payload possibly incomplete, e.g. a storm text batch with `customerIds: []`). A rerun of such an expired plan fails with a 400 on `steps.N.payload`, and a rerun of a plan where the owner switched a step off silently turns it back on.

**Decision (Ruling AX-P, narrowed by controller Ruling BC):**
- Every task type except `storm_plan`: a rerun copies each step's on/off state. A step with `enabled = false` is passed to `createTask` with `off: step.errorPlain ?? <the copy's generic switched-off reason>`, so its payload is kept as given (no 400) and it stays off. Steps that were on are re-checked as today.
- `storm_plan`: a rerun re-drafts through S1's `draftStormPlan(deps, companyId, companyStormId, now)` with fresh storm and customer data (it rebuilds switched-off steps with their reasons and re-runs the pause check), instead of copying the old payload. Find the company storm from `company_storms.plan_task_id = <old task id>`. `draftStormPlan` runs its own transactions and needs `deps`, so the branch lives in the rerun route (`apps/api/src/routes/approvals.ts` or wherever POST …/rerun is handled), before the generic `rerunTask`. Paused Storm Scout → 409 "Storm Scout is paused. Turn it back on in Agents to draft a plan." (copy approved, keep it in the API's message constants). The response keeps the rerun route's shape (the new task id). S1 is extending `replaceDraftPlan` / `draftStormPlan` to replace expired and declined plans as well as drafts; if on this base the replacement refuses an expired or declined old plan, extend the replace check (after Task 19 it is `lockStormPlan` in packages/db/src/repos/storm-plans.ts, then `supersedeDraft` in tasks/service.ts, then `linkStormPlan`; keep the lock order company_storms before agent_tasks) to accept `draft`, `expired` and `declined` old plans (never running/approved/done), and record it in the report as a merge rule to reconcile with S1's version.

**Do:** the storm_plan branch and the change in `rerunTask` (read the `off` handling in `createTask` and `listSteps` to get `enabled`/`errorPlain`); tests (db-backed, CI only) in `apps/api/test/task-events.test.ts` or the existing rerun tests: an expired draft with one switched-off step (incomplete payload) reruns into a draft whose copy of that step is still off with the same reason, and the other steps are on; the events of the rerun stay as today (`created` by the user). Storm plan: an expired storm plan with a switched-off step reruns without a 400 into a fresh draft (via draftStormPlan) whose off steps are rebuilt with reasons; a paused Storm Scout gives 409 and no task.

**Files:** `apps/api/src/tasks/service.ts`, the rerun route, `packages/db/src/repos/storm-plans.ts` (only the replace check, if needed), `apps/api/src/storm/plan.ts` (only if needed), and their tests.
