**Claims guard (`claims-guard.ts`):**
```ts
export type ClaimsGuardHit = GuardHit | 'deductible' | 'insurer_contact';
export function claimsGuard(text: string): { ok: boolean; hits: ClaimsGuardHit[] };
```
- Splits the text into sentences (`.`, `!`, `?` followed by space or end) and runs D1's `guardSentence(sentence, { repName: null })` on each, collecting its hits; plus, on the whole lower-cased text, `deductible` when it mentions waiving, covering, paying, absorbing, rebating or "taking care of" a deductible (e.g. "we'll cover your deductible", "deductible waived", "no out of pocket", "free roof" together with insurance/claim, "upgrade paid by insurance"), and `insurer_contact` when the text offers to contact or negotiate with the insurer or adjuster on the homeowner's behalf ("we'll call your insurance", "we will talk to the adjuster for you", "we'll negotiate with your insurer"). Asking the homeowner for their claim number, insurer name or adjuster date is allowed and must not hit (test it). `ok` = no hits; hits are de-duplicated, in first-found order.
