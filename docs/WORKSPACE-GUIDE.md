# Use Roofline

Double-click **Start Roofline.command** to open the app. Keep its terminal window running.

1. On **Today**, choose a property from the map or search for its address. **Save property** keeps it in your saved list.
2. Choose **Create estimate**. Existing measurements reopen automatically; otherwise choose **Get roof measurements**.
3. Select the correct roof. Check the material, building height and old roof layers. Expand the extra measurement fields when needed.
4. Choose **Continue to customer** and add a name. Phone and email are optional.
5. Choose **Continue to review**, check the details, then **Save & preview proposal**. Download the PDF from the preview.

The current EagleView connection uses Omaha test properties. Those proposals are for practice and stay in draft. Real customer quoting requires production access or measurements you supply yourself.

Use **Pricing** to enter your own material and labor costs. The starting prices are examples. Use **Company settings** to personalize new proposals. Existing proposals keep their original prices and company details.

**Check recent hail** shows NOAA’s public report locations for the selected date. Reports cover noon UTC to noon the following day and do not establish roof damage. **Back to properties** returns the map to Omaha.

Use the crosshair control to fit the properties currently listed. The expand control opens a larger explorer on desktop; press Escape to close it. Saved pins are green and the selected pin is gold. **Satellite view** and **Directions** open Google Maps for the chosen property. A completed roof report shows measured surface area and pitch in the property card; select the correct roof in the estimate.

**Proposals** opens your job board. Each column shows its proposal count and value. Open a card to review it and record the next stage. **List** switches to the table. Search by property, customer or proposal number. Open value includes draft, ready and sent proposals; it is not collected revenue. Practice proposals are labeled.

Use **Add follow-up** on Today, or the plus button on a proposal card, to save a reminder date and note. Click its date to reschedule, or the checkmark to complete it. These reminders are saved in this workspace. They do not send email or text messages. Due dates use the US Pacific calendar, matching proposal dates.

The top-bar search or **⌘K / Ctrl+K** finds your properties, customers and proposals. Type an address or proposal number, then click a result. Arrow keys and Enter also work. Escape closes the search.

Optional provider setup and developer tools are under **Connections**. The core estimate workflow does not require the command line.
